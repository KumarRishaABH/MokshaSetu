THE 3-CONFIDENCE CASCADE
We don't surveil for death — there's no bulk registry feed. We act in proportion to how sure we are.
🟡

1 · Suspicion

Inactivity · missed check-in

→ We ask the account holder first ("are you there?"). No condolences, nothing irreversible — it could just be a dead phone.

🟠

2 · Assertion

Nominee reports · insurer claim · bank flag

→ An accountable human says so → Tier-1 reversible protection begins (SIM no-recycle, fraud-watch) + gentle WhatsApp outreach.

🟢

3 · Proof

DigiLocker certificate + Aadhaar match

→ The state's own record — the only thing that unlocks anything irreversible (money, deletion), after a waiting period.

Insight from the offsite

Two users, not one

The single biggest reframing of the day: "the survivor" is not one person. Two users, opposite in every dimension — so they need different front doors.

🌱

The Planner

Proactive · younger · digitally native · low urgency. Signs up for the wish & message feature — it's about love and control, not death. This is how the vault gets populated years before it's ever needed.

Front door: the emotional product (free, viral, self-serve).

🕊️

The Survivor

Reactive · often 55+ · low digital ability · 48-hour urgency. The median case is a grieving widow who won't install a fintech app mid-mourning. She needs a voice, in her language, and is often set up by a younger relative.

Front door: Saarthi + an institution she already trusts.

💡 Why this decides the company: the Planner is acquired years before value; the Survivor needs value before she's heard of us. Bridging that gap is exactly why distribution runs through institutions (next section), not consumer ads.
The crux · where it lives or dies

The two-tier trigger

A false positive digitally kills a living person; a false negative lets a SIM recycle to a stranger. So we split every action by reversibility — fast where it's safe, slow where it's final.

⚡

Tier-1 · Protective

FAST · REVERSIBLE · LOW-PROOF

On a soft signal (nominee report + hospital/cremation proof) we take only non-destructive, reversible steps within hours:

  • Reinforce the 90-day SIM no-recycle window
  • Pause subscriptions · flag institutions for fraud-watch
  • Freeze outbound activity — nothing deleted, nothing moved

If it's false, everything reverses. This is the "magic moment."

🔒

Tier-2 · Consequential

SLOW · IRREVERSIBLE · 3-FACTOR

Money instructions, permanent deletion & formal claims fire only on the full trigger:

  • DigiLocker death certificate (state source of truth)
  • + Aadhaar match of the deceased
  • + Nominee co-authentication
  • + Waiting period & notification to all nominees

Slow is correct here. Sunlight + delay removes the fraud incentive.

Trust is the product

The zero-knowledge promise — and how it survives attack

Ilya's paradox: "if you truly can't read the vault, how does a nominee ever open it after death?" Resolved with threshold cryptography, not a marketing promise.

🙈
We can't read your vault
The key is split by Shamir secret-sharing (e.g. 3-of-5). No single party — including Virasat — can reconstruct alone. Below threshold the plaintext is mathematically unrecoverable.
🔑
We don't warehouse your keys
Financial discovery runs through Account Aggregator at the moment of need — we don't store account numbers or credentials. The vault mainly holds the map, instructions & wishes.
⚖️
We can't be forced to open it early
Reconstruction is gated on the verified Tier-2 trigger. Even under subpoena, even if breached, there is nothing to hand over before death is proven. Lost shares are handled by social recovery.

Market fit

Distribution is the strategy

Consumer-direct alone is a ~₹190 cr app, not a moonshot. The high-value, high-urgency Survivor can't be reached by ads — so the institution she's already dealing with is the channel. Distribution is simultaneously revenue, acquisition, and moat.

🛡️

Insurer-at-claim

HIGHEST CONVICTION · PILOT #1

At the death-claim moment the family is already discovering assets. The insurer offers "we'll help you settle everything, not just our policy" — better claims NPS for them, the highest-value user for us, paid by them.

🏦

Bank dormant-reunification

PILOT #2

"We'll reconnect your deceased/dormant accounts to heirs — cutting your UDGAM exposure and servicing cost." Tests willingness to pay on the money side.

Prove it or kill it

The first 90 days

We validate the three assumptions that can end the company before we over-build the fancy parts.

Kill-Assumption #1A regulated institution will pay to distribute this. → Two paid pilots (insurer + bank).
Kill-Assumption #2Big Tech won't ship a good-enough India feature. → Own the cross-institution execution they won't touch.
Kill-Assumption #3Account Aggregator works post-death (or via pre-death consent). → Regulator read + manual fallback.
🚦 Kill gate: if after two serious paid pilots no institution will pay and no family will pay the concierge fee, the thesis is wrong — and we stop, rather than limp along as a consumer app pretending to be a moonshot. Non-negotiable: zero false-positive irreversible actions. Ever.
